Agent Fi

A local-first AI assistant for Mac, built for program and project managers. Chat with a fully local model, or bring your own cloud provider — your data only leaves your Mac when you say so.

What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Local-first, cloud-optional Chat fully offline with a local model via Ollama, or bring your own OpenAI or Anthropic key — including custom, self-hosted endpoints.
Built for PM work One-click briefings, action items, status updates, meeting prep, and notes, right from chat.
Program tracking A living risk register and decision log the agent helps you keep current.
Document-grounded answers Import PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files; answers are grounded in what's actually in them.
Durable memory Facts persist across sessions, with automatic session summaries and full control to forget anything, any time.
Jira & Confluence Read and write issues and pages from chat, with every write gated behind your confirmation.
MCP support Connect your own MCP tool servers and extend what the agent can do.
Real exports Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, and Graphviz diagrams — genuine files, not text dumps.
Consent-gated by design Nothing beyond your message and recent conversation reaches a cloud provider without your explicit approval.
Every write is audited A durable, rotating, clearable audit log records what the agent did and why.
🔒

Local-first, and you're always in control.

Agent Fi can run entirely offline with a local model — nothing ever leaves your Mac. If you connect a cloud provider, only your typed message and the recent conversation are sent by default. Document text, saved memories, calendar events, reminders, and your display name are only ever included if you explicitly approve it in Settings, and you can withdraw that approval at any time. A full right-to-forget lets you clear memories or factory-reset the app whenever you want.
