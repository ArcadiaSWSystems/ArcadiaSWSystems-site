Privacy Policy

Arcadia SW Systems Inc.  ·  Last updated: August 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Arcadia SW Systems Inc. ("we", "us", "our") and the apps we publish — including QR Cute, Checkpoints, PixelPulse Groovebox, and CityGrid — as well as this website. None of our apps require an account, and none of them collect personal information about you. Most of our apps run entirely on your device and never contact a server at all; CityGrid is the one exception, described below.

Where a practice is specific to one app — such as an optional permission or integration — that is called out below.

Apps Covered by This Policy

  • QR Cute — custom QR code generator for iOS
  • Checkpoints — local version history for folders, for Mac
  • PixelPulse Groovebox — 8-bit and lo-fi music groovebox for Mac
  • CityGrid — live, open-data map of Toronto (web app at citygrid.ca)

Data We Collect

None of our apps require an account, login, or any personal information such as your name or email address.

QR Cute, Checkpoints, and PixelPulse Groovebox collect no data at all — they run entirely on your device and never send anything to a server. They do not track your location, use analytics or crash-reporting tools, or display advertising.

CityGrid is a live web app with a server backend that serves public open-data feeds (transit, weather, closures, and similar civic data). It does not collect personal data either, but — unlike our other apps — it does use:

  • Cookieless, first-party analytics — aggregate usage metrics, without raw IP addresses, search text, third-party trackers, or personal profiles, to understand overall reach
  • Anonymous push notifications — if you opt in to a transit alert, your browser creates a push subscription used only to deliver that alert; it's tied to your browser, not to an account or identity

None of our apps use third-party advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Device

This section applies to QR Cute, Checkpoints, and PixelPulse Groovebox. CityGrid is a hosted web app rather than a local app, so it's covered separately above and below.

These apps store the content you create locally on your device only:

  • QR Cute — the QR code configurations you save (content, style settings, and any custom logo image), stored using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • Checkpoints — the version history of the folders you choose to track, stored as local file snapshots
  • PixelPulse Groovebox — the music projects and patterns you create, and any WAV files you export

This data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere by us. If you enable iCloud backup or iCloud Drive on your device, this data may be included in your backups according to Apple's standard iCloud policies, which you control through your device settings.

Permissions & Optional Access

Some apps request optional access to power specific features:

  • Clipboard (QR Cute) — when QR Cute launches or returns to the foreground, it checks your clipboard for a URL and offers to paste it automatically. This is read locally and never transmitted. On iOS 16 and later, the system displays a notification whenever an app reads the clipboard.
  • Photo Library (QR Cute) — used only if you choose to add a custom center logo to a QR code. This access is optional; you can use the app without granting it.
  • Contacts (QR Cute) — QR Cute does not access your contacts. It only includes contact details you manually type into a vCard-style QR code.
  • Folder Access (Checkpoints) — Checkpoints only reads and writes within folders you explicitly add as a tracked project. It does not access other files on your Mac.
  • Browser Notifications (CityGrid) — if you opt in to a transit alert, CityGrid asks for permission to send browser push notifications. This is anonymous and tied to your browser subscription, not to a personal account, and you can revoke it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Camera — none of our apps access the camera.

Claude / MCP Integration (Checkpoints)

Checkpoints offers an optional integration with Claude Desktop, and other Model Context Protocol–compatible clients, so you can manage your version history through conversation. This integration is off unless you set it up yourself. When you use it, information you ask about — such as checkpoint labels, dates, or file differences — is shared with the MCP client and AI provider you've connected, and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. We do not receive or store this information.

In-App Purchases

Some of our apps, such as QR Cute, offer an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Arcadia SW Systems Inc. does not receive or store any payment information. CityGrid is free to use and has no purchases of any kind.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. None of our apps have user accounts or advertising, and none collect personal information.
